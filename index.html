PROJECT 01HARDWARE · ARDUINO

Arduino
Solar Tracker

A solar tracker that adjusts a solar panel's position to face a light source, controlled by an Arduino.

Highlights

  • Sensors detect and compare light intensity.
  • The Arduino reads the sensors and adjusts the panel's orientation.
  • Wiring, code and testing come together in one working build.
↗

What I learned
How a programmed system can respond to its surroundings, and how testing improves a design.

PACKAGING MOCKUP / AKEJA SALTED BANANA CHIPS
PROJECT 02DESIGN · PACKAGING

Chips Packet
Design

Packaging for Akeja banana chips, designed for a local shop. The goal was a fresh, simple look that feels natural and local.

Design choices

  • Soft green pouch with hand-drawn spiral motifs for a handmade feel.
  • Palm-grove and farm illustration along the base, showing where the chips come from.
  • Close-up chip and a clear “Salted” label so the flavour reads at a glance.
↗

Outcome
The shop later moved to transparent packaging to save cost, so the design was not printed. It still took a brief from idea to finished mockup.
